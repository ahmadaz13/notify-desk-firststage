# Notify Final Source Of Truth Matrix

This matrix records the production source of truth for Notify V1 business and financial values. Presentation layers may format and display these values, but must not become an authoritative calculator.

| Metric / Value | Canonical Source | Current Consumers | Deprecated / Non-Authoritative Alternatives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plan availability and catalog identity | `Plan`, `Service`, `CommercialCatalogController`, `CommercialPricingService` | Commercial catalog screen, subscription start and plan-change selectors | Hardcoded package options, legacy service-price assumptions |
| Monthly and annual recurring price | `PlanPrice` rows selected through `PlanPriceService::activeEffectivePrice()` or effective plan-price relationships | `BillingController`, `SubscriptionController`, client subscription UI, commercial catalog | Global default price fallback, UI-entered recurring price, hidden annual-discount calculation |
| Setup fee, branch quantity and additional branch price | `PlanPrice` persisted minor-unit fields and invoice snapshots created by billing services | Subscription start, renewal generation, invoice lines, commercial catalog display | Blade formulas, JavaScript calculators, legacy subscription pricing fields |
| Explicit discount and tax on issued invoices | Persisted `Invoice` and `InvoiceLine` snapshot fields created by `InvoiceService` / `SubscriptionBillingService` | Client billing workspace, collections, finance reports, accounting journals | Global tax or discount settings as overrides after issue, recomputing issued totals from current catalog |
| Subscription period and renewal eligibility | `SubscriptionBillingService`, `subscriptions`, `subscription_billing_periods` | Subscription billing dashboard, client billing workspace, scheduled/manual renewal actions | Legacy `payment_schedules`, date math in Blade |
| Annual payment terms and installment dates | `SubscriptionBillingService` and `PaymentScheduleService` over one effective annual `PlanPrice` and one issued annual `Invoice`; V2 schedule amounts use integer minor units | Paid-subscription preview, client billing workspace, immutable contract snapshot, renewal generation | Treating installments as monthly subscriptions, dividing price in Blade/JavaScript, separate installment receivables or invoices |
| Scheduled plan change, cancellation and reactivation | `SubscriptionBillingService` and subscription event/state records | Client billing workspace, subscription billing dashboard, SaaS metrics events | Direct subscription field mutation from UI |
| Invoice subtotal, discount, tax and total | Persisted `Invoice` / `InvoiceLine` minor-unit columns created by `InvoiceService` | Client billing workspace, collections, finance exports, accounting postings | Rebuilding invoice totals from current plan price, cash received, or recognized revenue |
| Receivable outstanding, overdue, partial balance and customer credit | `ReceivableService` using issued invoices, allocations, credit applications, refunds and reversals | Collections screen, client billing workspace, finance AR aging and customer credits | Legacy or V2 payment schedules as receivables, invoice total minus raw payments in Blade |
| Payment allocation and unallocated balance | `PaymentAllocationService` with `ReceivableService` checks | Collections forms, client billing workspace, accounting reconciliation | Client-side allocation math, direct edits to payment balance |
| Payment reversal, allocation reversal and refunds | `CollectionCorrectionService`, `RefundService`, append-only reversal/refund records | Collections, client billing workspace, cash movement and accounting services | Deleting payments/allocations or mutating historical payment rows |
| Cash inflow and outflow events | `CashMovementService`, `cash_movements` | Financial accounts screen, cash flow, accounting event posting | Payment totals, dashboard liquidity formulas, bank balance entered in UI |
| Financial account balance | `FinancialAccountBalanceService` over cash movements | Financial accounts screen, finance dashboard, reconciliation services | Stored display balance outside movement ledger |
| Internal transfer and transfer reversal | `FinancialAccountService`, `FinancialTransfer`, reversal records and cash movements | Financial accounts screen, cash flow and accounting event posting | Treating transfers as revenue/expense or changing company total cash |
| Journal posting | `JournalPostingService`, `AccountingEventPostingService`, `BillingAccountingService`, `JournalEntry`, `JournalLine` | Accounting screen, accounting reconciliation, finance reports | Blade-generated journal values, direct journal-line edits |
| Trial balance and ledger | `AccountingReportService` | Accounting screen | Summing journal lines in Blade |
| Accounting periods | `JournalPostingService`, `AccountingPeriod` | Accounting period controls and posting guards | UI-only period status |
| Accounting reconciliation | `AccountingReconciliationService` | Accounting screen, golden flow tests | One-off reconciliation formulas in controllers or views |
| Revenue-recognition schedules and periods | `RevenueRecognitionService`, `RevenueRecognitionSchedule`, `RevenueRecognitionPeriod` | Accounting screen, finance deferred/recognized revenue reports | Recognizing revenue from payments, invoice issue alone, or SaaS MRR |
| Recognized revenue | `RevenueRecognitionService`, revenue-recognition journals and `FinancialStatementService` reports | Accounting, finance, executive dashboard | Cash collected, invoice totals, dashboard gross revenue |
| Deferred revenue | `RevenueRecognitionService::operationalDeferredRevenueMinor()` and `FinancialStatementService::deferredRevenueReport()` | Accounting, finance, executive dashboard | Manual remaining-balance formulas in Blade |
| Credit-note recognition adjustment | `RevenueRecognitionService::creditNoteRecognitionSplit()` and related adjustment records | Credit-note issue flow, accounting, finance reports | Reducing revenue by UI calculation only |
| MRR, ARR and movement metrics | `SaasMetricsService` backed by `subscription_metric_events` and V2 subscription periods | SaaS metrics, executive dashboard, exports | Invoice totals, payments, setup fees, tax, one-time work, legacy ARR formulas |
| Cash collected for SaaS dashboard comparison | `SaasMetricsService::cashCollectedMinor()` using V2 payments in period | SaaS metrics, executive dashboard | Recognized revenue or MRR treated as cash |
| Profit and Loss | `FinancialStatementService::profitAndLoss()` over accounting/revenue/expense sources | Finance screen, finance export, executive dashboard | Legacy dashboard gross revenue and operational-cost formulas |
| Cash Flow | `FinancialStatementService::cashFlow()` over cash movements | Finance screen, finance export | Payments minus expenses in Blade |
| Balance Sheet | `FinancialStatementService::balanceSheet()` over accounting balances and approved operational projections | Finance screen, finance export | Market-valuation multiplier, legacy investment/capital formulas |
| AR aging | `FinancialStatementService::arAging()` using `ReceivableService` projections | Finance screen, finance export | `payment_schedules` aging |
| Operating expense totals | `OperatingExpenseService`, V2 `Expense` records and accounting postings | Operating expenses, finance reports, accounting | Legacy `expenses.store`, direct dashboard expense writes |
| Capital funding and fixed assets | `CapitalManagementService`, funding/fixed-asset records, cash/accounting services | Capital management, finance reports | Legacy `investments` and `capital_expenses` as active authority |
| Client lifecycle and subscriber transition | `ClientOperationalWorkflowService`, `SubscriptionBillingService::startPaidSubscription()` | Dashboard, client workspace, operational queues, billing workflow | CSV subscriber import, legacy client conversion, manual stage/status writes |
| Partner/referral attribution | `Partner`, `ClientPartnerAttribution`, and `ClientPartnerAttributionService`; `clients.partner_id` is synchronized legacy compatibility | Public referral intake, Client Workspace, internal Partner management | Partner login, partner-scoped authorization, ownership inferred from referral metadata |
| Partner commission projection | `PartnerCommissionService` over V2 payment receipt, payment reversal, and refund `CashMovement` records, using the Client attribution basis-point snapshot | Internal Partner detail page | Invoice totals, unpaid receivables, legacy payment totals, Partner model earned-share accessors, Blade calculations |

## Audit Notes

- `resources/views/accounting/index.blade.php` now consumes revenue-recognition schedule projections from `RevenueRecognitionService::dashboard()` instead of calculating recognized/deferred schedule values in Blade.
- `resources/views/clients/import.blade.php` presents CSV import as prospect-only. Backend compatibility still accepts legacy subscriber hints, but `CsvImportService` imports them as prospects and creates no financial records.
- Deprecated compatibility routes remain registered where documented, but active UI should prefer their authoritative replacements.
- Legacy partner dashboard/layout and Laravel starter welcome view are retained because the project documentation says not to remove legacy compatibility or orphan files without an approved archival plan.
